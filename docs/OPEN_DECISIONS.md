## Navigation milestone proposals

The [design and readiness assessment](NAVIGATION_CONTROLLER_READINESS.md) and [ADR-0007](decisions/ADR-0007-opt-in-local-navigation-controller.md) remain PROPOSED for navigation. The human explicitly accepted stop/fault policies H1-H3 on 2026-11-13; only that subset of #24-25 is accepted. Items #4, #5, #11, #12, #14 and the general event-handler policy in #18 remain OPEN. No hardware, navigation algorithm or production-safety claim follows from these software policies.

H1-H3 establish inhibition before fallible work, immediate ordinary cancellation stops, truthful acknowledgement of failed delivery, and bounded emergency zero attempts. Motion faults remain visible in service health; no new operating-mode transition is introduced for faults outside supported safe-state commands. Operational recovery, real-adapter timestamp acceptance, independent watchdogs and physical stopping remain unresolved.

Explicit mock assumptions only: state and target share one consistent local frame; heading conversion and rudder sign are declared by the fixture; state samples are coherent for the scripted test; parameters are test inputs. These assumptions establish no localisation capability, hull dynamics, propulsion mapping or production threshold. Missing speed, geographic position and uncertainty remain unknown. A command-generation test with scripted positions is not evidence that commands caused movement or that zero propulsion physically stops a vessel.

When a decision is made, write an ADR in `decisions/`, update this table, and change the relevant code and tests.
